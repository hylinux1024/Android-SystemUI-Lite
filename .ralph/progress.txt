=== Iteration 10 — US-009: Screen Recording tile starts/stops a real MediaProjection session [2026-07-04]
- data/ScreenRecorderController.kt (new): Koin singleton owning the live recording
  session — MediaProjection, MediaRecorder (AGC+1hour max duration), and a
  VirtualDisplay bound to the recorder surface. start(resultCode=Int, data: Intent)
  builds a MediaStore Video entry under RELATIVE_PATH=Movies/ScreenRecordings with
  IS_PENDING=1, hands the ParcelFileDescriptor to MediaRecorder.setOutputFile,
  wires the projection callback (onStop => stop), and flips isRecording=true only
  after recorder.start() succeeds. stop() tears down recorder → display → projection
  and clears IS_PENDING so the file shows in gallery/files. onConsentResult handles
  both grant and deny/cancel; failure paths release the projection + delete the
  MediaStore placeholder. MIC audio is attached when RECORD_AUDIO is granted,
  otherwise the session records video-only (graceful degradation).
